Corrections get priority. If a figure on this site doesn't match its source, we want to know.

How to reach us

Volt & Ledger shares a contact desk with its sister site. Send your message through the nexusEVnews contact page and mention Volt & Ledger so it reaches the right place.

What to include

  • For a correction: the page, the figure you think is wrong, and the source you're comparing against.
  • For a question about your own bill: your car, your state, and your utility's rate if you know it.
  • For a privacy request: what you'd like us to do. See our privacy policy.

What we can't do

We can't tell you which car to buy, negotiate with your utility, or give tax or legal advice. Our calculators are estimates, and your utility bill is always the final word.
