About Volt & Ledger

Volt & Ledger answers one question as honestly as we can: what will this actually cost you?

Most EV advice is either a brochure figure or a guess. We do something narrower. We take numbers that government agencies and published studies already report, put them in calculators you can use in ten seconds, and show the source of every figure so you can judge it yourself. Three tools so far: a finder that matches first-time buyers to cars by what they actually care about, a calculator for what charging costs per month, and one for how many miles an EV drives before it repays the carbon cost of building its battery.

Where our numbers come from

  • EV efficiency: the EPA's official ratings, from the vehicle data file published on fueleconomy.gov.
  • Electricity prices: the U.S. Energy Information Administration's average residential rate for each state.
  • Gas prices: the EIA's weekly fuel price update.
  • Grid emissions: EPA's eGRID database, by state.
  • Battery manufacturing emissions: the ICCT's 2025 lifecycle study, built on Argonne National Laboratory's GREET model, shown as a range because no single official figure exists.

We refresh these monthly and spot-check them by hand against the source before publishing. The how we calculate page shows the formula and the current data dates.
