When does an EV pay back its manufacturing carbon?

An EV starts with a bigger carbon footprint because of its battery, then emits less every mile. This calculator estimates the mile where the two lines cross, using EPA grid data, IPCC lifecycle factors, and published battery studies. Every number shows its source. Nothing you enter is saved.
