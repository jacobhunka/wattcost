How this works, and what it can't tell you

Loading…
  • Range, efficiency, body style, drive and overnight charge time come from the EPA's published vehicle data, for every current EV.
  • Price, fast-charging speed, seats, cargo, towing, plug type and dashboard controls are entered by hand from each manufacturer's own page, with the page and date recorded. EPA publishes none of them, and aggregator sites disagree: one search for a single car's starting price returned four different figures. A car whose field is blank is labelled "not checked yet" rather than guessed at.
  • Reliability is a 1-to-5 judgment recorded here with its reasoning and a link, not a rating copied from anyone. Consumer Reports and J.D. Power own their ratings; we don't republish them. Free federal complaint and recall records are at NHTSA.
  • Winter range uses AAA's cold-weather testing and Recurrent's real-world study.
